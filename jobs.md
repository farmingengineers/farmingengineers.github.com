Join our experienced team of farmers for a growing season! We grow diverse
certified organic vegetable crops, cut flowers, and potted plants / plant
starts on 6 acres, including three high tunnels, and sell at two Saturday
farmers markets almost year round.  Our farm is about an hour northwest of
Indianapolis.

We primarily hire early in the year, January through March.
